# TCC Formatting Review: ABNT Standards vs ESALQ Manual Requirements

**Purpose**: Verify whether the ESALQ TCC Manual follows ABNT NBR 14724:2011 (and updates) or has institution-specific deviations.

## Executive Summary

The **ABNT NBR 14724:2011** (and corrigendum 2020) is the official Brazilian standard for academic work presentation. However, many institutions, including **USP/ESALQ**, have specific TCC manuals that may:
1. Follow ABNT strictly
2. Add institution-specific requirements
3. Have minor deviations from ABNT

**Action Required**: Compare the `Manual de Instruções e Normas para Trabalhos de Conclusão de Curso (242).pdf` against the ABNT checklist below.

---

## ABNT NBR 14724:2011 - Core Formatting Requirements

### 1. Page Setup (Preparo da Página)

| Element | ABNT NBR 14724:2011 Requirement | Typical TCC Manual Deviation | Status |
|---------|--------------------------------|------------------------------|--------|
| **Paper size** | A4 (210mm x 297mm) | Usually follows ABNT | ✅ Standard |
| **Margins** | Left: 3cm; Right: 2cm; Top: 3cm; Bottom: 2cm | May vary (check TCC manual) | ⚠️ Verify |
| **Font** | Times New Roman 12pt or Arial 12pt | May specify one font | ⚠️ Verify |
| **Line spacing** | 1.5 lines | Some TCCs require 1.5 or double | ⚠️ Verify |
| **Paragraph indent** | 1.25cm first line | Usually follows ABNT | ✅ Standard |
| **Page numbers** | Bottom center, from first page | May differ for pre-textual | ⚠️ Verify |
| **Header** | Not typically required | TCC may require header | ⚠️ Verify |

**Action**: Open TCC Manual Section "Formato do Trabalho" or "Apresentação" and compare each element.

---

### 2. Pre-Textual Elements (Elementos Pré-Textuais)

| Element | ABNT Requirement | Typical ESALQ TCC Requirement | Priority |
|---------|------------------|------------------------------|----------|
| **Cover (Capa)** | Required: Institution, Course, Title, Author, Advisor, Date | May add ESALQ logo, specific wording | ABNT + TCC |
| **Title page (Folha de rosto)** | Extended cover with more details | Usually follows ABNT | ABNT |
| **Catalog sheet (Ficha catalográfica)** | CIP - Required for library | Mandatory for USP | Both |
| **Dedication** | Optional | Usually optional | TCC preference |
| **Acknowledgments** | Optional | Optional but recommended | TCC preference |
| **Abstract (Resumo)** | Required in Portuguese | Mandatory | ABNT |
| **Keywords (Palavras-chave)** | 3-5 keywords | Mandatory | ABNT |
| **Abstract (English)** | Required | Usually required for MBA | TCC requirement |
| **Keywords (English)** | 3-5 keywords | Usually required | TCC requirement |
| **List of figures** | If more than 5 figures | Follow TCC manual | TCC |
| **List of tables** | If more than 5 tables | Follow TCC manual | TCC |
| **List of abbreviations** | If needed | Optional | TCC |
| **Table of contents** | Required | Mandatory | ABNT |

**Critical Check**: The TCC Manual may specify **exact wording** for cover page elements (e.g., "Dissertação apresentada..." vs "Trabalho de Conclusão de Curso apresentado...").

---

### 3. Textual Structure (Estrutura Textual)

| Element | ABNT NBR 14724 | TCC Manual Typical | Notes |
|---------|----------------|-------------------|-------|
| **Chapter numbering** | Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3...) | Usually follows ABNT | Standard |
| **Section numbering** | 1.1, 1.1.1, 1.1.1.1 | Usually follows ABNT | Standard |
| **Introduction** | First chapter, no number | Usually follows ABNT | Verify |
| **Chapter titles** | ALL CAPS, bold, centered | May vary (bold, uppercase) | Verify font style |
| **Section titles** | Title Case, bold, left-aligned | Verify capitalization | Check manual |
| **Subsections** | Sentence case, bold | Verify format | Check manual |

**Common TCC Deviations from ABNT**:
- Introduction may be numbered as "Chapter 1" instead of unnumbered
- Chapter titles may use Title Case instead of ALL CAPS
- Section numbering may have different depth limits (e.g., max 3 levels)

---

### 4. Post-Textual Elements (Elementos Pós-Textuais)

| Element | ABNT Requirement | TCC Requirement | Status |
|---------|------------------|-----------------|--------|
| **References** | Mandatory, ABNT NBR 6023:2018/2023 | Mandatory | ABNT + TCC |
| **Appendix** | Optional | Optional | TCC |
| **Annex** | Optional | Optional | TCC |
| **Glossary** | If needed | Rarely required | TCC |
| **Index** | Optional | Rarely required | TCC |

**Important**: ABNT distinguishes between:
- **Appendix**: Author's own supplementary material
- **Annex**: External/secondary supplementary material

---

### 5. Citation and References (Citações e Referências)

| Element | ABNT NBR 6023:2018/2023 | TCC Manual | Priority |
|---------|------------------------|------------|----------|
| **Citation format** | (Author, Year) or Author (Year) | Usually follows ABNT | ABNT |
| **Direct quote** | "Text" (Author, Year, p. xx) | Usually follows ABNT | ABNT |
| **Reference style** | Author-date system | Usually follows ABNT | ABNT |
| **Reference list order** | Alphabetical by author | Usually follows ABNT | ABNT |

**Critical**: Some TCC manuals require numbered citations [1], [2] instead of author-date. This would be a **major deviation** from ABNT.

---

### 6. Tables and Figures (Quadros, Tabelas e Figuras)

| Element | ABNT Requirement | TCC Deviation | Check |
|---------|------------------|---------------|-------|
| **Caption position** | Top for tables; Bottom for figures | May specify differently | ⚠️ |
| **Numbering** | Continuous or per chapter (1, 2, 3 or 1.1, 1.2) | Usually follows ABNT | ✅ |
| **Source note** | Mandatory for all tables/figures | Mandatory | ABNT |
| **Title** | Above table; Below figure | Verify position | ⚠️ |
| **Frame** | Tables have frames; Figures may not | Verify | ⚠️ |

**ABNT Terminology**:
- **Quadro**: Textual data in framed format
- **Tabela**: Numerical data
- **Figura**: Graphs, charts, maps, images

TCC Manual may use these interchangeably or have specific requirements.

## Critical Elements to Verify

### HIGH PRIORITY (Will block submission if wrong)

1. **Citation Style**
   - ABNT: Author-date (Silva, 2020)
   - If TCC requires numbered: [1], [2] — this affects entire bibliography
   
2. **Margens**
   - ABNT has specific requirements
   - TCC may have different specs for binding
   
3. **Font and Size**
   - Must be readable for committee
   - Some fonts may not be acceptable
   
4. **Abstract Length**
   - ABNT: No specific word count
   - TCC may specify 200-250 words
   
5. **Cover Page Wording**
   - ABNT: Flexible
   - TCC: Usually exact wording required

### MEDIUM PRIORITY (May require revision)

1. **Table/Figure caption positions**
2. **Line spacing**
3. **Header/footer requirements**
4. **Page numbering start point**
5. **List of tables/figures threshold**

### LOW PRIORITY (Cosmetic)

1. **Chapter title capitalization**
2. **Section numbering depth**
3. **Dedication/acknowledgments placement**

## ABNT Standards Reference

For official ABNT requirements, consult:

| Standard | Title | Year |
|----------|-------|------|
| **NBR 14724:2011** | Trabalhos acadêmicos — Apresentação | 2011 (Corrigendum 2020) |
| **NBR 6023:2018/2023** | Referências — Elaboração | 2023 update |
| **NBR 6024:2012** | Numeração progressiva das seções | 2012 |
| **NBR 10520:2023** | Citações | 2023 |
| **NBR 6034:2023** | Resumo e abstract | 2023 |

**Note**: ABNT standards are periodically updated. The TCC Manual may reference an older version.

## Quick Decision Matrix

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| TCC Manual says X, ABNT says Y | **Follow TCC Manual** (institutional requirements prevail) |
| TCC Manual is silent, ABNT specifies | **Follow ABNT** (national standard) |
| Both specify, but differ | **Follow TCC Manual** (stricter/more specific wins) |
| Neither specifies | **Follow ABNT** (national standard) or ask advisor |

**Bottom Line**: The TCC Manual requirements **always take precedence** over general ABNT standards for thesis submission at ESALQ.
